# Finding Boring Business Ideas: The "Selling Shovels" Framework

Finding great boring business ideas is not about brainstorming random apps. It is a systematic process of identifying unsexy friction in existing, profitable workflows.

---

## 1. The "Selling Shovels" Mental Model

During the Gold Rush, the most consistent profits were made not by gold miners, but by merchants selling shovels, pickaxes, jeans, and wheelbarrows.

When observing any emerging trend or large industry:
1. **Who is spending money trying to make money?**
2. **What repetitive, painful, or compliance tasks slow them down?**
3. **What data format conversions, reports, or integrations do they require?**

### Archetype Transformations

| Hype / Trend | The Fragile Gold Miner | The Boring Shovel Seller |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Generative AI** | AI copywriting wrapper | LLM cost tracker / token budgeting proxy |
| **Creator Economy** | New video sharing app | Subtitle SRT cleaner / multi-track audio extractor |
| **Remote Work** | Virtual office metaverse | State-by-state payroll compliance calculator |
| **E-commerce** | Dropshipping store | Shopify return policy generator & sync |
| **Agencies / B2B** | "Full service AI agency" | Client reporting PDF generator from Google Sheets |

## 2. The 5 Best Hunting Grounds for Boring Ideas

### A. Regulatory & Compliance Changes
Governments and industry bodies regularly mandate new rules (GDPR, cookie laws, accessibility, state sales tax, e-invoicing).
- People must comply or face fines.
- Budget is non-discretionary.
- Examples: Cookie banner widgets, Pix invoice generators, ADA web accessibility checkers.

### B. Marketplace App Stores
Browse ecosystem app stores with high transactional intent:
- Shopify App Store
- WordPress Plugin Directory
- Chrome Web Store
- Slack App Directory / Notion Integrations
- Look for 3-star reviewed apps with thousands of installs: users need the tool, but hate the current implementation.

### C. File & Data Transformations
Businesses constantly move data between incompatible systems:
- PDF to CSV / Excel converters.
- Bank statement parsers for accounting software.
- Video / audio transcoders and metadata strippers.
- Real estate listing syndicators.

### D. Niche B2B Directories & Curated Marketplaces
- High-intent search terms: "[service] for [specific industry]".
- Event vendors for community organizers, drone operators for agriculture, certified welders for maritime shipping.
- Monetization: featured listings, job postings, buyer inquiries.

### E. Internal Agency Tools turned Micro-SaaS
- Agencies build custom scripts to serve clients (client onboarding forms, SEO audits, competitor scraping).
- Productizing that internal script removes custom labor and provides instant recurring revenue.

## 3. Extracting Founder Fit & Constraints

Before generating ideas, extract 3 core parameters:
1. **Technical & Domain Skills**: What stacks do you know? What industries have you worked in?
2. **Weekly Time Allocation**: 5 hours/week vs 20 hours/week changes the target archetype.
3. **Target Audience Preference**: Solo-developer tools, SMB owners, or specialized freelancers.
