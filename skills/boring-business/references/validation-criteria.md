# Validation Criteria & Two-Tier Staged Funnel

To avoid spending 30-45 minutes and $20+ validating ideas that should have been rejected in 5 minutes, use the **Two-Tier Validation Funnel**:

```text
IDEATION (5-10 candidate ideas)
    ↓
TIER 1: Quick Validation (10-15 minutes) -> Filter out 70% of non-starters
    ↓
TIER 2: Deep Validation (30-45 minutes)  -> Full competitive scorecard & GTM roadmap
    ↓
SELECTION: Build high-confidence MVP
```

---

## Tier 1: Quick Validation (10-15 Minutes)

### Primary Question
> **"Does this idea demonstrate enough basic signal and feasibility to justify deep analysis?"**

### The 4 Quick Checkpoints

1. **Search Demand Signal**:
   - Extract 5-7 core search terms.
   - Sum search volume: Target is **1,000+ monthly searches** (US or target geo).
   - Commercial intent vs informational: Are people searching to solve/buy or just learn?
2. **Keyword Difficulty / SERP Real Estate**:
   - Are the top 5 results dominate by DR 85+ enterprise sites, or are there low DR niche blogs, forum threads, or dated tools?
3. **Clear Problem-Solution Fit**:
   - Can you explain the problem and the exact automated solution in two sentences?
4. **Solo / High-Automation Feasibility**:
   - Can an MVP be built in 1-2 weeks and run with near-zero ongoing manual fulfillment?

### Decision Matrix
- **Score >= 70 (or 4 green flags)**: 🟢 **GO** -> Advance to Tier 2 Deep Validation.
- **Score 50-69**: 🟡 **PIVOT** -> Adjust keyword angle or audience before advancing.
- **Score < 50**: 🔴 **NO-GO** -> Disqualify immediately.

---

## Tier 2: Deep Validation (30-45 Minutes)

### The 5 Core Weighted Pillars

| Pillar | Weight | Key Question | 5/5 Signal | 1/5 Signal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Search Demand** | **25%** | Are buyers actively searching? | 10k+ monthly searches, growing YoY | <100 searches/mo, declining |
| **2. Distribution Potential**| **25%** | Can we reach customers organically?| 3+ viable zero-CAC channels (SEO, marketplaces, communities) | Paid ads only, high CAC |
| **3. Organic Growth** | **20%** | Will customers share or retain? | Natural artifacts, programmatic loops, public output | Embarrassing, one-time, secret use |
| **4. Automation Level** | **15%** | Can it run on autopilot? | 95%+ automated, self-service, simple stack | Heavy custom onboarding, manual labor |
| **5. Willingness to Pay** | **15%** | Will they pay meaningful recurring money? | B2B, critical workflow pain, paid competitors exist ($50-$500/mo) | Consumer "nice to have", free alternatives ($0-$5/mo) |

### Weighted Score Formula
```text
Overall Score = (Search Demand × 0.25) +
                (Distribution × 0.25) +
                (Organic Growth × 0.20) +
                (Automation × 0.15) +
                (Willingness to Pay × 0.15)
```

### Final Decision Thresholds

| Overall Score | Final Verdict | Actionable Next Step |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **4.0 - 5.0** | 🟢 **BUILD IT** | Build MVP immediately. Target launch in 14 days. |
| **3.5 - 3.9** | 🟡 **PRE-SELL & VALIDATE** | Set up landing page, collect emails or 10 pre-orders. |
| **3.0 - 3.4** | 🟡 **INVESTIGATE** | Conduct 5 customer discovery interviews to clarify pain. |
| **2.5 - 2.9** | 🟠 **PIVOT** | Rethink angle, customer segment, or distribution channel. |
| **< 2.5** | 🔴 **ABANDON** | Reject idea. Preserve time and capital for better opportunities. |
