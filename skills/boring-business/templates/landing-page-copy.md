# Landing Page Copy Template for Boring Businesses

This template provides the structure for high-converting landing pages optimized for boring businesses (practical, problem-first, not trend-first).

## Why This Structure Works for Boring Businesses

Unlike flashy SaaS products that can rely on novelty, boring businesses need to:
1. **Establish credibility immediately** - You're solving a known problem, not creating a new category
2. **Be extremely specific about the problem** - Target customers know exactly what frustrates them
3. **Focus on time/money saved** - Not on "revolutionary" features
4. **Provide social proof** - Boring businesses need trust more than excitement
5. **Make the CTA practical** - "Start Free Trial" beats "Join the Revolution"

---

## 6-Section Structure

### Section 1: Hero (Above the Fold)

**Purpose**: Hook visitors in 3 seconds by clearly stating what problem you solve and for whom.

**Components**:
1. **Headline** (5-10 words)
   - State the outcome, not the feature
   - Use specific numbers when possible
   - Address the primary pain point

2. **Subheadline** (10-15 words)
   - Clarify who this is for
   - Add a secondary benefit or time/cost savings

3. **Primary CTA Button**
   - Action-oriented but not pushy
   - Show the first step clearly

4. **Visual/Screenshot** (if applicable)
   - Shows the product in action
   - Or illustrates the problem being solved

**Boring Business Examples**:

✅ **Good (Event Vendor Directory)**:
- Headline: "Find vetted event vendors in under 1 hour"
- Subheadline: "Transparent pricing, no ghosting, curated for Bay Area event planners"
- CTA: "Search 500+ Vendors"

✅ **Good (Invoice Template Tool)**:
- Headline: "Professional invoices in 5 minutes, not 50"
- Subheadline: "For freelancers who need to get paid, not fiddle with formatting"
- CTA: "Create Your First Invoice"

❌ **Bad (Too Vague)**:
- Headline: "The future of event planning"
- Subheadline: "Revolutionary platform for modern professionals"
- CTA: "Get Started"

**Formula**: [Specific Outcome] in [Time Frame] for [Target Customer]

---

### Section 2: Problem (The Pain)

**Purpose**: Agitate the pain points so visitors think "Yes, that's exactly my problem!"

**Components**:
1. **Section Headline**
   - Empathetic acknowledgment of frustration
   - Example: "We get it. Finding reliable vendors shouldn't take 10 hours."

2. **3-5 Specific Pain Points** (bullet list)
   - Use customer language (from validation research)
   - Be specific, not generic
   - Include tangible costs (time, money, frustration)

3. **Current Bad Solution**
   - What they do now that sucks
   - Why existing alternatives fail

**Boring Business Examples**:

✅ **Good Pain Points (Event Vendor Directory)**:
- "You spend 5-10 hours researching venues on Yelp, only to find half don't respond"
- "Pricing is hidden until after a 30-minute sales call"
- "Reviews are unreliable - incentivized and outdated"
- "Vendors ghost you 2 weeks before the event"
- "No way to filter by your actual needs (AV setup, dietary restrictions, etc.)"

✅ **Good Pain Points (Invoice Tool)**:
- "You waste 30 minutes formatting invoices in Word"
- "Clients pay late because your invoice looks unprofessional"
- "You forget to invoice for small projects (lose $1000+/year)"
- "Tracking which clients paid is a spreadsheet nightmare"

**Formula**: List 3-5 pain points in this format:
- "[Specific action] takes [time/cost] and results in [bad outcome]"

---

### Section 3: Solution (How It Works)

**Purpose**: Show how your product directly solves each pain point. Keep it simple.

**Components**:
1. **Section Headline**
   - Transition from problem to solution
   - Example: "Here's how we make vendor search actually work"

2. **3-4 Core Features** (as benefits, not features)
   - Map directly to pain points from Section 2
   - Use "You get X, so you can Y" format
   - Include specific time/money savings

3. **How It Works** (3-step process)
   - Simple, linear workflow
   - Each step is 1-2 sentences max

**Boring Business Examples**:

✅ **Good Solution Copy (Event Vendor Directory)**:

**Core Benefits**:
- **Curated Vendors Only**: Every vendor is vetted. No unresponsive vendors, no surprises. Find options in 15 minutes, not 5 hours.
- **Transparent Pricing Upfront**: See exact pricing before contacting anyone. Budget with confidence.
- **Real Event Planner Reviews**: Only verified event planners can review. No fake reviews, no incentivized ratings.
- **Response Guarantee**: Vendors respond within 24 hours or they're removed. No more ghosting.

**How It Works**:
1. **Search** - Filter by event type, budget, location, and specific needs (AV, dietary, capacity)
2. **Compare** - See pricing, availability, and verified reviews side-by-side
3. **Book** - Contact vendors directly or book through the platform with payment protection

✅ **Good Solution Copy (Invoice Tool)**:

**Core Benefits**:
- **Templates That Look Professional**: 20+ designer templates. Your invoices look like a $500/hour consultant sent them.
- **Send in 5 Minutes**: Pre-fill client data, add line items, click send. Faster than opening Word.
- **Automatic Payment Reminders**: Set it once, never chase invoices again. Get paid 30% faster on average.
- **Track Everything Automatically**: See who paid, who's overdue, total revenue - all in one dashboard.

**Formula for each benefit**:
- **[Feature Name]**: [What it does] [Specific outcome/time saving]

### Section 4: Features (The Details)

**Purpose**: For visitors who need more convincing, provide detailed feature breakdown.

**Components**:
1. **Section Headline**
   - Example: "Everything you need to [achieve outcome]"

2. **6-8 Features** (grid or list format)
   - Short feature name (2-4 words)
   - 1-sentence explanation focused on benefit
   - Optional: icon or small visual

3. **What's NOT Included** (optional but powerful)
   - Be upfront about limitations
   - Builds trust for boring businesses

**Boring Business Examples**:

✅ **Good Features List (Event Vendor Directory)**:

- **Advanced Filters**: Search by cuisine, capacity, AV setup, accessibility, dietary restrictions
- **Verified Reviews**: Only actual event planners who booked through us can review
- **Pricing Transparency**: See base rates, per-person costs, and common add-ons upfront
- **Availability Calendar**: Real-time availability, book before competitors grab your date
- **Vendor Response Tracking**: See average response time before contacting
- **Comparison Tool**: Compare up to 5 vendors side-by-side
- **Save Favorites**: Bookmark vendors for future events
- **Direct Booking**: Book and pay through platform with payment protection

**What's Not Included** (builds trust):
- ❌ We don't plan your event for you (we're a directory, not a concierge)
- ❌ We don't negotiate pricing (you deal directly with vendors)
- ❌ We only cover the Bay Area currently (expanding to LA in Q2 2025)

**Formula**: [Feature Name]: [1-sentence benefit in plain English]

### Section 6: Call-to-Action (The Ask)

**Purpose**: Make it dead simple to take the next step. Remove all friction.

**Components**:
1. **Headline**
   - Reinforce main benefit
   - Create urgency if appropriate (but don't fake scarcity)

2. **CTA Button** (prominent, action-oriented)
   - Use first-person language ("Start My Free Search")
   - Show what happens next

3. **Below-CTA Copy** (reduce risk)
   - No credit card required
   - Cancel anytime
   - What they get immediately

4. **Final Objection Handler** (optional)
   - FAQ-style quick answers
   - Pricing transparency
   - Money-back guarantee

**Boring Business Examples**:

✅ **Good CTA (Event Vendor Directory)**:

**Headline**: "Stop wasting hours on Yelp. Find your venue in 15 minutes."

**CTA Button**: "Search 500+ Vetted Vendors" (not "Sign Up" or "Get Started")

**Below CTA**:
- ✅ Free to browse and search
- ✅ Only pay when you book through us (5% platform fee)
- ✅ Cancel bookings up to 7 days before event
- ✅ Full refund if vendor doesn't show

**Final Objection Handlers**:
- "How much does it cost?" → Free to search. Only pay 5% platform fee when you book.
- "What if the vendor cancels?" → Full refund + we help you find a replacement ASAP.
- "Do I have to book through you?" → Nope. You can contact vendors directly for free.

✅ **Good CTA (Invoice Tool)**:

**Headline**: "Send your first professional invoice in 5 minutes"

**CTA Button**: "Create My First Invoice Free"

**Below CTA**:
- ✅ No credit card required
- ✅ Free plan includes 5 invoices/month
- ✅ Export to PDF immediately
- ✅ Upgrade only if you need more

**Formula for CTA**:
- Headline: [Restate main benefit] in [time frame]
- Button: [Action verb] + [What they get]
- Below: [3-4 risk reducers with checkmarks]

## Copy Best Practices for Boring Businesses

### Voice & Tone

**DO**:
- ✅ Use specific numbers (5 hours → 15 minutes)
- ✅ Use customer language (from validation interviews)
- ✅ Be conversational but not cutesy
- ✅ Focus on time/money saved, not "innovation"
- ✅ Show, don't tell (screenshots, examples)

**DON'T**:
- ❌ Use buzzwords (revolutionary, game-changing, innovative)
- ❌ Make vague claims (better, faster, easier - without specifics)
- ❌ Over-promise (10x your productivity, never worry again)
- ❌ Use jargon unless your audience uses it
- ❌ Create fake urgency (only 3 spots left!)

### Headline Formulas for Boring Businesses

1. **Time-Saving Formula**:
   - "[Outcome] in [X minutes] instead of [Y hours]"
   - Example: "Professional invoices in 5 minutes, not 50"

2. **Problem-First Formula**:
   - "Stop [painful current state]. Start [desired state]."
   - Example: "Stop chasing payments. Start getting paid on time."

3. **Outcome-First Formula**:
   - "[Specific outcome] for [target customer]"
   - Example: "Vetted event vendors for Bay Area planners"

4. **Before/After Formula**:
   - "[Bad current state] → [Good new state]"
   - Example: "Yelp ghosting → Guaranteed 24-hour responses"

### Length Guidelines

- **Headline**: 5-10 words (fit in mobile screen)
- **Subheadline**: 10-15 words
- **Pain points**: 1-2 sentences each (5-7 per bullet)
- **Feature descriptions**: 1 sentence (10-15 words)
- **Testimonials**: 2-3 sentences (30-50 words)
- **CTA button**: 2-5 words

### Mobile-First Writing

- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
- Bullet points over long blocks of text
- Section headlines every 2-3 mobile screens
- White space between sections
- CTA buttons thumb-friendly size

## A/B Testing Variations

Generate 3 headline variations for testing:

**Variation A: Outcome-Focused**
- "Find vetted event vendors in 15 minutes"

**Variation B: Problem-Focused**
- "Stop wasting 10 hours researching vendors on Yelp"

**Variation C: Specificity-Focused**
- "500+ vetted Bay Area event vendors. Transparent pricing. Guaranteed responses."

Test with real traffic, pick winner based on conversion rate (not personal preference).

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Mistake #1: Being Too Clever**
Bad: "Event planning, elevated"
Good: "Find vetted event vendors in 15 minutes"

❌ **Mistake #2: Feature Vomit**
Bad: "Advanced ML-powered search with real-time availability sync"
Good: "See which vendors are available for your date before contacting"

❌ **Mistake #3: No Specifics**
Bad: "Save time and money on events"
Good: "Find venues in 15 minutes instead of 5 hours, save $4K on average"

❌ **Mistake #4: Generic Social Proof**
Bad: "Thousands of happy customers"
Good: "500+ event planners. 2,000+ successful bookings. 4.8/5 average rating."

❌ **Mistake #5: Weak CTA**
Bad: "Sign Up" or "Learn More"
Good: "Search 500+ Vetted Vendors" or "Create My First Invoice Free"

## Usage in Landing Page Creator Agent

When the Landing Page Creator agent generates copy, it should:

1. **Read** `project.json` for:
   - Brand tone (casual vs professional)
   - Voice attributes
   - Words to avoid
   - Target audience persona
   - Primary pain points

2. **Query RAG** for:
   - This template structure
   - Copywriting best practices (AIDA, PAS, BAB formulas)
   - Boring business case studies with successful copy

3. **Extract from validation**:
   - Primary keywords (use in headlines)
   - Top 3-5 pain points (use in Section 2)
   - Competitor weaknesses (use for differentiation)
   - Target customer language

4. **Generate**:
   - 6 complete sections following this template
   - 3 headline variations (A/B/C)
   - Copy matching brand tone from project.json
   - Specific numbers from validation research

5. **Output**:
   - `landing-page/copy.md` - Structured markdown
   - `landing-page/a-b-variants.md` - Headline variations

**Quality check**: Does the copy pass the "3-second test"? Can a visitor understand what this solves and for whom in 3 seconds of scanning the hero section?
